// 03. FEATURED PROJECT

Post Accident Survivor Detection System

December 2025 – January 2026 • IoT & Embedded Systems

An IoT-based accident detection and survivor identification system designed to detect victims in accident-prone environments and communicate critical information to nearby hospitals and healthcare centres in real time.

The Problem

Accidents can require immediate medical intervention, but delays in identifying survivors and communicating accident information can increase response time.

The Solution

The system uses sensors and embedded technologies to detect accident-related conditions and identify potential survivors, then automatically transmits relevant information to healthcare facilities.

Key System Capabilities

Sensor-based detection

Continuous environmental and physical impact monitoring.

Survivor identification

Presence verification to distinguish critical victims.

Real-time monitoring

Instantaneous edge processing of accident triggers.

Automated hospital alerts

Direct emergency telemetry dispatched to medical centers.

Reduced manual intervention

Autonomous reporting without bystander reliance.

Real-time information transmission

Low-latency telemetry packets sent over network channels.
